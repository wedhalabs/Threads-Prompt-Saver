Each post gets its own folder here, named after its prompt, holding its images, videos and prompt.txt.
Read prompts from images (optional)
When a post publishes its prompt as text, that text is saved exactly as written. When it doesn't, a vision model looks at each saved image and does one of two things.

If the prompt is printed on the image — the carousel slides that share a prompt as a picture — it is copied out word for word, under PROMPTS READ FROM THE IMAGES. That is the author's own prompt.

If nothing is written on the image, the model describes a prompt that would produce something similar, under RECONSTRUCTED PROMPTS, clearly marked as a guess. The two never share a section.

Leave blank and nothing is sent anywhere and nothing costs money. Filled in, every image of a promptless post costs one API call — an 11-image carousel is 11 calls.
Any OpenAI-compatible endpoint works. The key is stored on this machine only and is sent to no one but the URL above.
